## Word tasks (Wendler et al.): translation, repetition, cloze

P = full-string probability under the layer-l lens, averaged over items the model gets right at the output (greedy). `latent` = translation of the output word in the other language; `ctl` = translation of a different word. Latent index = max over layers 1-15 of P(latent) - P(ctl) per item, then mean. Commit layer = first layer FROM WHICH P(output string) stays at or above half of its output-layer value.
